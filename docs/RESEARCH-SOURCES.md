## Missing evidence
Willingness to pay, actual customer interviews, measured task/translation accuracy, model runtime costs, supplier quotes, platform access approvals, actual CAC/churn and signed partnerships are not established.
No exhaustive market or financial feasibility study was completed. Preserve this uncertainty rather than converting targets into facts.
