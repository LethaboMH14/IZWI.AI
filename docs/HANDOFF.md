## What is not implemented
No production app, integrations, social-account connections, authentication, payments, usable media editor or AI workers. Product screens are mockups. A generated poster is not a source implementation.

## Important qualifications
55 subscriptions is a six-month pilot target, not break-even. Prices and model-compute budgets require usage tests. Neither creator declarations nor content provenance establish legal ownership. Translation requires per-language evaluation and human review. Dubbing requires explicit permissions and is later scope. Lower-spec local processing requires hardware benchmarks.
