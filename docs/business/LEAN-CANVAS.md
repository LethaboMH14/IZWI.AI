# Sonar lean canvas — working transcription
Source: assets/source/sonar-team-profile.pdf, dated 15 November 2026. This is a structured handoff transcription, not a replacement for the original PDF. Numerical research claims below require source-level verification before reuse.

## Problem
South African creators face fragmentation, language/accessibility constraints and difficulty converting work into sustainable income. Separate editing, translation, publishing, analytics, rights and monetisation tools create repeated work and expense.

## Customer segments
Primary: South African independent podcasters, YouTubers, short-form creators, educators and storytellers, especially 18–34; freelance editors and small studios with recurring client content.
Secondary: agencies, production companies, musicians, filmmakers, animation studios; universities, NGOs, broadcasters and training institutions.
Early adopters already publish regularly and experience repeated workflow bottlenecks.
Initial geography: Johannesburg/Gauteng, then wider South Africa, Southern Africa and international markets.
Buyers: creators, studio owners, agency managers and institutional procurement teams.

## Unique value proposition
One Voice. Every Language. Every Market.
Turn one creative idea into multiple market-ready assets in an AI-powered connected workspace. The slogan is a vision, not proof of every language/model being supported.

## Solution
Agentic creative studio; multilingual localisation; connected audience intelligence; approved distribution integrations; creator declarations/agreements, licensing and storefronts; hybrid cloud/local/BYOK model adapters.
MVP: upload → generate/edit clips → review multilingual captions → export → prepare distribution.
Advanced dubbing, commerce and wider integrations follow progressively.

## Intended advantage
Native-speaker-reviewed evaluation and localisation workflows, creator relationships, reusable preferences/templates and consented performance feedback, reliable orchestration and community partnerships. These are intended defensibility, not existing proprietary assets or signed partnerships. No exclusive ownership of open-source models is claimed.

## Channels
Phase 1: studio/editor/community demos, campus media, short tutorials, workshops and referrals.
Phase 2: production/agency/training partners, ambassadors, case studies and approved tool integrations.
Phase 3: regional language communities, international agencies and institutional sales.
Demonstrate a useful first export before trying to convert repeat users.

## Metrics
North star: creators producing a useful export or published asset each week.
Six-month targets (not traction): 800 registered; 300 first usable exports; 55 paid subscriptions; 30% of activated users return within 30 days; 300 completed exports per month; 20 interviews; five repeat-using studio customers.
Commercial: conversion, MRR, CAC, retention/churn, contribution/customer and cloud cost/completed task.
Quality: time to first export, edit/translation acceptance, render retries/failures, security/data-loss incidents. Use privacy-conscious analytics.

## Costs
Fixed monthly proposal: founder/engineering R50000; part-time design/QA/content R12000; support/community R6000; baseline infrastructure R5000; marketing/partnerships R8000; legal/admin/security R4000. Total R85000.
Variable: compute, storage, rendering, transfer, processing/refunds, incremental support/infrastructure.
Hosted compute budgets per subscriber: Creator R25; Studio R110; Agency R390. These are model limits, not measured task costs. Local/BYOK does not eliminate support or infrastructure costs.

## Revenue
Free R0: local editing, compatible BYOK, basic exports, limited hosted trials.
Creator R149/month: cloud allowance, exports, workflow automation and supported publishing.
Studio R499/month: collaboration, more allowance, shared projects/client workflow.
Agency R1499/month proposed: multi-client management, larger allowance and advanced permissions.
Additional: prepaid usage credits; proposed 7% commission on qualifying marketplace sales/licences with separate disclosed processing; custom institutional contracts; optional qualified managed services.
Exact allowances, VAT treatment and pricing require validation.

## Claims in source requiring validation
The PDF cites Census 2022 English-at-home 8.7%, GHS 2024 fixed-home internet 17.4%, and a Lit.africa Q3 2026 creator-index claim (24900 registered creators; 51.3% of income respondents below R10000/month). Preserve these as source claims, not verified findings. Check definitions, denominator, survey period, availability and sampling before publishing charts or treating income as a market-wide salary.
