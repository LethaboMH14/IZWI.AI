## 2. The dictated instruction to IZWI

Sbu speaks this to IZWI after the stream ends: phone in hand, or leaning into the laptop mic.

**Honesty note (read before you shoot):** today IZWI's speech-to-text (whisper.cpp) does **not** transcribe isiZulu or Setswana speech. So the instruction is **mostly English, with greetings and short phrases** that code-switch. On screen, the instruction appears as **typed text** in the IZWI assistant box. If we show it as live voice input, the frame must be labelled **"Concept"**. Translating captions into isiZulu or Setswana needs a connected translation provider, and the ad will say so in its small print.

### 2a. Full version (radio/long cut, target 14 s, ~34 words)
> "IZWI, sawubona. Take today's stream and find the three funniest moments — the kota, the P.O. box, and the dance. Make them vertical, with captions in English, isiZulu, and Setswana for my Pitori people. Ungakhohlwa — check Lethabo's consent before anything goes out. Ke a leboga."

(*Ungakhohlwa* = "don't forget" (isiZulu). *Pitori* = Pretoria (slang). *Ke a leboga* = "thank you" (Setswana).)

### 2b. Short version (for the 20 s pitch video, target 6 s, ~16 words)
> "IZWI — sawubona. Find the funny bits. Captions in English, isiZulu, Setswana. Check Lethabo's consent. Ke a leboga."

## 4. Performance, framing and gestures

**Voice**
- Sbu (streamer): high energy, fast, then a sudden deadpan on "government department."
- Manager (Sibusiso): drop the pitch, slow down, sigh before "Kulungile." Disbelief on "Futhi?!", then a long pause before "unekati kuphela."
- Lethabo: innocent and unbothered. Chew lightly on the funeral line, not on the "food" line, because that line has to be clear.
- Narrator: smile while speaking, no announcer voice. Slow down on "Amplified."

**Framing (video, livestream scene)**
- 16:9 landscape, 1920×1080, 30 fps. Camera at eye level, desk-mounted webcam feel. Sbu centre-left, empty chair right for Lethabo to enter.
- Keep a 15% safe margin on every side. IZWI crops 9:16 clips out of this frame, so **keep both faces inside the centre 56% of the width** (the vertical crop zone).
- Warm practical light (ring light plus a lamp). No purple LED backgrounds, to match the ivory, amber and charcoal brand.
- Keep the second-screen laptop visible on the desk for the "End stream" click.

**Gestures to hit (editors cut on these)**
1. Catching the ring light (line 1)
2. Plate landing on the desk (line 2)
3. The box flap opening, held 1 s toward camera (line 4)
4. The can crack, held near the mic (line 6)
5. The dance: same move, both of you, 3 counts
6. The End-stream click, with the finger visible

## 5. Takes and file naming

Record **3 full takes** of the livestream, **3 takes of each dictation version**, and the radio ad **line by line, 3 takes per line**, plus 2 full run-throughs.

Name every file like this:
```
izwi_<piece>_<scene-or-line>_<speaker>_t<take>.<ext>
izwi_stream_full_both_t1.mp4
izwi_dictation_full_sbu_t2.wav
izwi_dictation_short_sbu_t1.wav
izwi_radio_L05_lethabo_t3.wav      (L01..L10 = table rows above)
izwi_radio_full_both_t2.wav
izwi_radio_narrA_lethabo_t1.wav    (narrA / narrB / narrC)
izwi_roomtone_30s.wav              (30 s of silence in the same room, required)
```
Audio: WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit if your recorder allows it (16-bit is fine), mono, peaks around −12 dBFS, no compression or effects. Video: native camera file, no filters.
